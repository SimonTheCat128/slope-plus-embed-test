Drive Mad

Fancade 4/5 1 984 votes

Drive Mad is a fun driving game where you steer your car across a long series of tricky and unpredictable tracks. Each stage throws something new at you: collapsing bridges, moving platforms, sudden drops, and tight jumps that require careful timing. Your main goal is to control your speed so the car doesn’t flip while still moving fast enough to clear obstacles. The further you go, the tougher and more creative the challenges become, turning every level into a small puzzle. Can you stay calm, keep the car balanced, and reach the finish line in every stage?
